Chicken Tikka Masala

Ingredients

  • Boneless chicken thighs, cubed
  • Yogurt
  • Garlic, minced
  • Ginger, grated
  • Garam masala
  • Cumin
  • Paprika
  • Salt
  • Tomato sauce
  • Heavy cream
  • Cilantro, chopped

Preparation

  1. Marinate the chicken in yogurt, garlic, ginger, garam masala, cumin, paprika, and salt.
  2. Grill or broil the chicken until cooked through.
  3. In a saucepan, combine the tomato sauce and heavy cream. Simmer until thickened.
  4. Add the grilled chicken to the sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Garnish with chopped cilantro.

Serving

  • Serve with basmati rice
  • Naan bread
  • Sliced onions and lime wedges

Notes

  • Adjust spice levels to your preference
  • Can use boneless, skinless chicken breasts instead of thighs
